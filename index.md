# Privacy Policy – Discord Bot

**Last Updated: November 20, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how our Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users interact with it.

The Bot provides Discord server management, moderation, automation, role management, and administrative features through Prefix Commands and Slash Commands.

## 2. Information We Collect
Depending on the features enabled by server administrators, the Bot may collect and process:

- Discord User IDs.
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs.
- Discord Channel and Role IDs.
- Moderation records, including warnings and moderation actions.
- Command usage information.
- Server configuration and settings.
- Message content when necessary for prefix commands, moderation, or automated responses.
- Member-related information required to provide specific bot features.

## 3. How We Use Information
Information is used exclusively to:

- Provide server management and moderation functionality.
- Process prefix and slash commands.
- Manage roles and permissions.
- Maintain moderation records.
- Provide automated responses and server utilities.
- Improve the Bot's reliability and functionality.
- Respond to user requests and support inquiries.

## 4. Data Storage
Some information may be stored using MongoDB or other secure storage systems to maintain bot functionality and preserve server configurations.

We do not intentionally collect information unrelated to the Bot's functionality.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the Bot, comply with applicable laws, or protect the security of our services.

## 6. Message Content
The Bot may process message content when required for prefix commands, moderation systems, automated responses, and other enabled features.

Message content is not intentionally used for advertising or unrelated purposes.

## 7. Presence Information
If Presence features are enabled, the Bot may process Discord presence information to provide relevant functionality.

Presence information is not used for unrelated purposes.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion
Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality.

Users and server administrators may contact the Bot owner to request access to or deletion of eligible stored information.

Requests will be reviewed and processed as appropriate.

## 9. Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Children's Privacy
The Bot is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children below the minimum age required to use Discord.

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
